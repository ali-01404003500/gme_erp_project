# Annual Earn Leave & Encashment

## Purpose

This document explains how annual earned leave is calculated and encashed based on attendance, ensuring employees understand their entitlement, the calculation method using monthly salary divided by 30, and the rule that no more than 60 days can be encashed during total service. It aims to ensure transparency, compliance with labor law, and fair leave encashment for all eligible employees.

## Eligibility

- Employees become eligible for annual earned leave encashment after completing at least 1 year of job.
- Over their entire job life in the organization, an employee can encash up to a maximum of 60 days of annual earned leave.
- After crossing 60 days (either by availing or encashment), the employee is no longer eligible for further annual leave encashment.

## Earned Leave Calculation Method

As per labor law:

- For every 18 days of presence, the employee earns 1 day of annual earned leave.

**Example from the uploaded calculation:**

| Item                | Days      |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Total year          | 365 days  |
| Weekly holidays     | 52 days   |
| Festival holidays   | 12 days   |
| Sick leave          | 2 days    |
| Casual leave        | 8 days    |
| Absence             | 5 days    |
| Total non-present days | 79 days   |
| Present days        | 365 - 79 = 286 days |

Then,

**Earned leave days:**
286 / 18 = 15.89 days

## Annual Leave Encashment Calculation

### Salary calculation:

- Use total monthly salary (default divided by 30, not by month days).

**Example:**

- Total salary: 14,247 BDT
- Daily salary: 14,247 ÷ 30 = 474.9 BDT

### Encashment value:

daily salary × Earned leave days = 474.9 BDT * 15.89 DAYS = 7546.16 BDT

## Additional Rule

The annual earned leave (encashable leave) is total earned leave ÷ 2 or Encashment value / 2

So if someone earns 15.89 days earned leave in a year:

Annual encashable leave = 15.89 ÷ 2 = 7.945 days OR 7546.16 / 2 = 3773.08 BDT

## Key Points

- Monthly salary is always divided by 30 to get daily salary.
- Leave encashment is only applicable up to the cumulative limit of 60 days.
- The employee must have at least 1 year’s tenure to get annual leave encashment.
- Annual earned leave is calculated based on attendance, but encashment is half of the earned leave.
